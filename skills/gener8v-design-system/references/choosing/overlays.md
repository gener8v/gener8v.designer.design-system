## Dialog

Stop the page for a decision the person has to make now.

**Use when**

- A confirmation before something irreversible — list what it will write in the body, and turn `dismissOnBackdrop` off.
- A short, self-contained task that must finish or be cancelled before the page is usable again.
- Give it a `tone` when the decision is destructive, so the surface says so and not only the prose.

**Not when**

- A longer panel of detail or navigation that slides in beside the page → `Drawer`. A drawer is a modal side panel with a pinned head and a scrolling body; a dialog is a centred decision.
- A few controls anchored to a button, with the page still usable → `Popover`. A popover is non-modal and does not trap Tab; a dialog takes the page over.
- An agent proposes a change in a conversation and a person must approve it → `ConfirmCard` (agents-and-live-work.md). The confirm card sits inline, stays as a record once answered, and states spend before the button.
- Reporting something that needs no answer → `Alert` (status-and-feedback.md). An alert can be read past; a dialog demands dismissal.
- Searching for a command or place across the app → `CommandPalette` (navigation.md). The palette is a dialog built for typing and choosing; do not rebuild it from `Dialog`.

**Confused with** `Drawer`, `Popover`, `ConfirmCard` (agents-and-live-work.md), `CommandPalette` (navigation.md)

React `Dialog` · hooks `useModalBehavior` · classes `.dialog` · shown in `design/components/feedback.html`

## Drawer

Slide a modal panel in from the side of the page.

**Use when**

- The site links behind a `Hamburger` on a phone (`side="end"`), or a rail that becomes a drawer on small screens (`side="start"`).
- Detail about one item — a source, a finding, a transcript — read beside the page, with `header` pinned and a `PagerChip` to step through the set.
- `size="lg"` for a reading width; it takes the whole screen on a phone.

**Not when**

- A short decision that blocks the page → `Dialog`. A dialog is a centred decision with actions; a drawer is a panel to read or move around in.
- The side pane should stay open while the person works in the page → `Split` (screens.md). A split pane is part of the layout and does not trap focus; a drawer is modal.
- A small panel anchored to the control that opened it → `Popover`. A popover floats beside its trigger and leaves the page usable.

**Confused with** `Dialog`, `Split` (screens.md), `Popover`, `Sidebar` (navigation.md)

React `Drawer` · classes `.drawer-panel`, `.drawer-scrim`, `.drawer-head`, `.drawer-body`, `.drawer-close` · shown in `design/components/navigation.html`

## HoverCard

Preview what a link, a name or an id leads to, on pointing or focus.

**Use when**

- A glance at what is behind a reference — a person behind a name, a requirement behind an id, a source behind a citation.
- Markers inside sanitised HTML you did not render in React: use `useDelegatedHoverCard` on the container.

**Not when**

- The person has to act inside the panel → `Popover`. Focus does not move into a hover card and Tab does not enter it; anything to press belongs in a popover.
- One line that describes a control → `Tooltip`. A tooltip is the control's description; a hover card is a preview of somewhere else.
- A citation marker in prose → `Citation` (reading.md). The citation draws the marker and its `SourceCard`; the hover card is only the surface it opens in.

**Confused with** `Tooltip`, `Popover`, `Citation` (reading.md)

React `HoverCard` · hooks `useDelegatedHoverCard` · classes `.hover-card` · shown in `design/components/live.html`

## Popover

Open a small panel of content or controls beside the button that opened it.

**Use when**

- A click opens a few controls or a short form anchored to its trigger — a date, a share setting, a run's detail.
- The page stays usable: Escape and a click outside close it, and Tab is not trapped.
- `size="lg"` for a panel with a reading width of its own that scrolls inside itself.

**Not when**

- A list of actions or choices → `Menu` (input.md). A menu has the menu-button keyboard contract and closes on choice; a popover is free content.
- A preview that opens by pointing or focus and needs no action inside → `HoverCard`. A hover card opens on dwell and focus; a popover waits for a click.
- One line describing a control → `Tooltip`. A tooltip is a description wired by `aria-describedby`, not a panel.
- The person must finish or cancel before going on → `Dialog`. A popover you cannot Tab out of is a dialog wearing the wrong clothes.

**Confused with** `Menu` (input.md), `HoverCard`, `Tooltip`, `Dialog`

React `Popover`, `PopoverTitle`, `PopoverActions` · hooks `useAnchoredPosition` · classes `.popover` · shown in `design/components/live.html`

## Tooltip

Describe a control in a line, on hover or focus.

**Use when**

- An icon button or a terse label needs a few words of description — "Copy link", "Last synced 4 minutes ago".
- Instead of a `title` attribute, which the operating system draws and touch and keyboards never reach.

**Not when**

- The words are the control's only name → `Button` (input.md). A tooltip describes via `aria-describedby`; an icon-only button needs its own `aria-label`.
- The hint is load-bearing — something the person must read to fill a field → `Field` (input.md). A field hint is always on screen; a tooltip is only there for someone who points at it.
- A preview of what a link or id leads to → `HoverCard`. A hover card holds a card of content; a tooltip holds a line.
- Controls or a form inside the floating panel → `Popover`. Nothing in a tooltip can be pressed.

**Confused with** `HoverCard`, `Popover`, `Callout` (status-and-feedback.md), `Field` (input.md)

React `Tooltip` · classes `.tooltip` · shown in `design/components/feedback.html`
