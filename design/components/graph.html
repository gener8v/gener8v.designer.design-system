Repulsion uses a Barnes–Hut quadtree, so it is O(n log n) rather than O(n²) — at 1,000 nodes that is the difference between 10,000 operations a frame and 500,000. Positions live in flat Float64Arrays, and the live component renders to canvas: 1,000 nodes plus edges would be roughly 4,000 DOM elements, which no browser composites at 60fps.

Radius is sqrt(degree), capped at 34px. The eye compares area, so linear scaling would make a 100-edge hub read as a hundred times more connected than a 10-edge one rather than ten. Link strength is divided by the lesser endpoint degree — without that, a hub with 349 edges takes 349 spring impulses per tick and the layout diverges to infinity.

Browse as list in the live component is the keyboard path, not a fallback: every entity, its kind, and what it connects to.
