Same four states and the same data-state, drawn as a filled span. A marker and its label are a pair, and the pairing breaks when a sequence spans a page — six stages on a wide screen put a quarter of the width between one circle and the next, and how far along the sequence is cannot be seen at all, because every circle but one is the same grey. Use the markers when each step is an act a reader performs; use the rail when the sequence is a position they are somewhere in.
