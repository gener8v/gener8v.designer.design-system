Every mark in the progress family is a tonal disc with one glyph from one set: done is a tick on the success tint, refused a cross on the danger tint, stopped short a ! on the warning tint, skipped a dash on a dashed disc. The current step is the one in the action colour. The glyph is centred on the marker by construction, not nudged.

Same states and the same data-state, drawn as segments of the family’s bar: the 4px track, the 2px gap and the state colours of a segmented bar. A marker and its label are a pair, and the pairing breaks when a sequence spans a page. Use the markers when each step is an act a reader performs; use the rail when the sequence is a position they are somewhere in.
