A matrix is presence on a grid: a real table, with every cell announced under its row and its column. The cells are one Tab stop and arrows move between them. It scrolls inside its own frame with the headings and the row labels pinned, so on a phone the page stays still.

Rules, not stripes — on a light ground a hairline is enough separation and striping doubles the noise. Headers are the mono voice. Figures are tabular and end-aligned, so digits line up down the column. Sort state lives in aria-sort and selection in aria-selected, so a screen reader and the styling read the same source.

The last one is a table somebody writes into. The keys down the left came out of the data and the columns marked Yours to fill are the facts no file will ever carry, so the head says who owes what. Enter and ↓ go down the column, ↑ goes up, ⌘D repeats the cell above, and each column offers back what it already holds — because the alternative is one territory acquiring three spellings. Cells are written on the way out rather than per keystroke.
