On unknowns

An unanswered control is evidence of a gap in the assessment, not evidence of compliance. The distinction is the whole point of the audit trail.

Tone is one vocabulary across .alert, .toast, .state and .callout. Danger and warning alerts take role="alert" and announce assertively; everything else is polite. The callout is a rule rather than a filled box — a box here would compete with the cards around it.
