Account Hierarchy

Three hierarchies are in use at once S4, and the reporting team rebuilds its own every quarter S7. A fourth was mentioned once and never produced S12 (source not found).

Each hierarchy is correct for the team that keeps it. The disagreement surfaces only at month-end, when the board pack rolls them up together.

The rail is held to the window and scrolls inside itself, so a contents list of seventy-eight sections reaches its last entry. The current entry is aria-current="location"; a long heading is held to two lines with the rest in its title. Reviewed marks are the caller's keys, so a mark can lapse when the section is rewritten. Below 1100px the rail goes above the sheet.

Rewritten in this run is a small dot, not a tick: the review box draws the tick. Where an entry is reviewed the dot is left out and the screen-reader words stay (compare 2.4 above, reviewed, with 2.4 in the plain list below).
