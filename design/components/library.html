A removal is struck through and an addition underlined; by line, the gutter adds a sign. The tint is the second signal, never the only one.

File type is a mono badge and a glyph, never a coloured chip — a dozen tinted extensions is the wall of colour this system exists to avoid, and colour-coding by extension fails anyone who cannot separate the hues. Folders are the one exception, because they are navigation rather than content. The reader caps its measure at 72ch; a document pane stretched across a 27-inch monitor is unreadable.
